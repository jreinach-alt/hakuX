A flicker detector for the Playable gate. `docs/testing/burst_capture.py` records a `screenrecord` burst from a held handheld. It holds the Nova panel's 60 Hz, 59-60 distinct frames/s on 60 fps titles; back-to-back screencap gets one frame per 0.7-1.3 s. `docs/testing/flicker_score.py` scores frame triples: a pixel blinks when frame N differs from both neighbours while the neighbours agree. The burst number is p90, and `p90 > 5` is FLICKER. A still screen is `unmeasured`, not clear. `docs/testing/flicker.md` is the one-line check before a Playable is accepted.

|---|---|---|---|

| Spikeout: Battle Street (ledger) | 3 | 0.0-0.12 | clear |

The defect: a rival car beside the camera is drawn on alternate frames while its shadow is drawn every frame. It is filed as a NEW ISSUE in OUTBOX. Changed after the first session, and recorded in NOTES: the burst number went from `rate` to `p90`, because Orta's legitimate explosions and white flash put `rate` as high as no-car RalliSport. Session 3, scored with the metric fixed, gives 14.6 and 14.1 against a negative maximum of 0.443. Limits: it sees only what is on screen (RalliSport's hold-play drives off alone and reads clean), objects under about 0.5% of the frame fall below the gate, and the replicates sit 2.8x over the gate.
