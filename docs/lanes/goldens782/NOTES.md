# goldens782: the Thor-made goldens on the Nova (#782)

Offline, $0 device time. The table is `TABLE.md` (generated by `make_table.py` from the dispatch titlestate store).

## What is true now

- **54 Thor-made goldens remain, not 55.** Forza's golden was re-harvested on the Nova (`5725499d3c7f`, source
  `nova-held.qcow2`), so it has already left the Thor set. The 55 in #782 counted it. The other 30 goldens are Nova-made
  (29 by image name, plus `4D530065`, whose source is `hdd-reset/nova-20261003T022340Z/pulled.img`).
- **Per title, the offline evidence is thin:**

| answer | count | titles |
|---|---|---|
| no (frame-confirmed loads on the Nova) | 2 | Castlevania `4B4E002D`, Spikeout `53450029` |
| yes, likely (`.sig` file in the golden) | 1 | `4D53001E` (Thor golden `6e97d00a8a46`: `global-data.sig`, `global-gameflags.sig`) |
| unknown | 51 | everything else |

  "No `.sig`" does not mean unsigned. Forza's signature sits in two places: `CarIcons.sig` (120 bytes) and a 20-byte
  trailer at the end of `Garage.bin`. The Thor and Nova `Garage.bin` differ in exactly those 20 bytes (offsets
  584448-584467) and in no others. A title can sign inside a plain data file, and the `.sig` test misses that.
- **No `rejected` record exists for any title on either device** (`devices/nova.json`, `devices/thor.json`: `rejected`
  is `{}`). Forza's damage was never written down, so the composer's rejection path (`titlestate.compose`, which skips
  a rejected golden and refuses a returning route for it) has never fired.
- **The signed body is plaintext, not encrypted.** Forza's `Garage.bin` entropy is 0.33 bits/byte; the difference
  is the 20-byte trailer, not the content.

## Option (a): re-sign with the Nova's key. Blocked; P about 0.1 today

What was tested, offline, against the Thor key:

- The Thor `eeprom.bin` is on disk (`wt/titlestate/scratch/desk/eeprom.bin`, md5 `f52cf53a...`). `hdd_key` is bytes
  28-43 (`hw/xbox/eeprom_generation.h`, `XboxEEPROM`). That gives `bc5454cc98ae4c96b5e8835feccfe304`.
- Hypotheses tested for Forza's Thor golden `a1baf745d557`: HMAC-SHA1 and SHA1 with `hdd_key`, `hdd_key+confounder`,
  the 20-byte `hash` field, and the whole EEPROM as key, each over `Garage.bin` (without its trailer), `Garage.dat`,
  `Garage.bin`+`Garage.dat`, and the reverse. The 20-byte trailer and each 20-byte chunk of `CarIcons.sig` were checked.
  **No hit.** So the scheme is not "HMAC-SHA1 over the file with the raw HDD key". The brief's assumption is not
  confirmed, and I did not find the real derivation in the repo (`hw/xbox`, `tools/`, `android/` have no save-signature
  code).
- The Nova's `eeprom.bin` (md5 `7eb04a87...`) is not on disk. Option (a) needs both keys, and the Nova's key can come
  only from a device pull of `files/x1box/eeprom.bin`. That is a device action, outside this lane's tools.

Titles cleared if it worked: up to 54. P is low because the algorithm is unknown and can't be checked without a reference
output: the Nova-side result would only show as "damaged" or not. P x titles about 5.

**What would unblock it:** the save-signature routine (the algorithm and message scope), or a Nova-signed/Thor-signed
pair of identical plaintext with a known key. Forza's pair is one such pair, but it does not tell us the algorithm.

## Option (b): per-device goldens. Mechanism exists; the observations do not. P about 0.9 per title; the cost is first-run time

- The composer already does what (b) describes: `titlestate.record(dev, tid, "rejected", run)` adds the golden to
  `rejected[tid]`, and `compose()` then drops it. A returning route for that title raises `NoGolden` and the lane has to
  run first-run instead. The first-run is the fallback, and it works per title only when the title's first-run route
  reaches play.
- So (b) needs no new code. It needs a `rejected` record for each title the first Nova returning run shows as
  damaged. Nothing detects "Player profile ... is damaged" automatically (`grep damaged` over `docs/testing/titles/`
  finds only the docstring). A detector would have to read the frame, and that belongs to the screen-reading agent
  (pathfind) or to the lane that runs the route.
- Blanket-rejecting all 54 is wrong. It would send 51 titles of unknown status to first-run, and first-run has its own
  failure rate (Forza's first-run gave up at 15 min; its golden came from a second pass).
- Titles cleared if applied to the 51 unknowns: about 0.9 x 51 = 46 titles lose the damage failure, at the cost of
  one first-run route each.

## Option (c): one `eeprom.bin` on both handhelds. A device change, not this lane's call

- Put one EEPROM on both. The 54 Thor-made goldens would then load on the Nova, and so would the Nova-made ones on the
  Thor. But the Nova-made signed goldens break on the Nova: Forza's `5725499d3c7f` is one (it reads as damaged once the
  Nova key changes), and up to 30 Nova-made goldens are at risk if they sign too.
- Titles fixed up to 54, titles broken up to 30 (1 confirmed). Proposed to lane.local, not done.

## What the next lane should not repeat

- Do not try another HMAC/SHA1 guess against the Thor key. The 75 combinations above gave no hit. The next attempt needs the
  real save-signature routine.
- Do not read the `.sig` test as "unsigned" for a title without one. The Forza trailer case shows the signature can
  be inside a data file.
- Do not blanket-reject all Thor goldens on the Nova. Each rejection sends a title to first-run.
