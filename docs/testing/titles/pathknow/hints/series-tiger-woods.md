# Tiger Woods PGA Tour 2003, 2004, 2005, 06, 07 (EA; 45410021 4541003A 45410054 4541007E 454100A4, Thor)
No route on this emulator yet. Every line below is general knowledge of these games, unverified here:
replace a line with what the screen showed when one of these titles reaches play.
- Expect EA SPORTS logos and an intro video (A or START), then a profile or save prompt (pub-4541.md).
- Fastest to a hole: Play Now, Quick Play or a single round (Stroke Play) with the default golfer and
  course. Avoid Create-a-Golfer, Career/Tour and Online: Create-a-Golfer is a long chain of sliders and
  keyboards.
- On a name keyboard, take the default name and look for DONE/OK (global.md, name_entry).
- Gameplay is a golfer standing at the ball with a HUD: club, wind, lie, distance and often a swing meter.
  A hole flyover or a scorecard before the shot is a cutscene or results: A skips it.
- The proof input: STICK:left or STICK:right turns the aim line or camera. The swing is the left stick
  pulled down and pushed up (2004 and later) or A presses on a meter (2003). An aim change is enough for
  rule 5.
