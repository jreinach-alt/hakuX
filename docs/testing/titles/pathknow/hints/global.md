# Hints for every title (each line names the titles it was seen in)
Inputs: UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT are the D-pad, one row per press; STICK:<dir>:<s> holds the left stick; RT:<s>/LT:<s> hold a trigger. If one of D-pad and stick does not move a cursor, try the other (GoldenEye: Rogue Agent 4541005D and Kabuki Warriors 43560001 moved only with the stick; BloodRayne 4D4A0001 only with the D-pad).
## intro_video, publisher_logo, black, loading
- Skip with A; if A changes nothing, try START once. A skipped Castlevania: CoD (4B4E002D), GTA: San Andreas (54540082) and Forza (4D53006E); only START skipped Sonic Heroes' Dolby logo (5345002B); only A skipped Crash Twinsanity's intro (56550036).
- One press per screen, then re-read. A press on a title's fade-in takes its default item (Castlevania took Continue that way): wait until the screen stops moving.
- Some videos cannot be skipped: after two presses with no change, stop pressing and re-read. Black (45410083) credits ~2 min and briefing ~2.5 min; Burnout 3 (4541005B) Race Training ~110 s; GoldenEye: RA briefing ~65 s; BloodRayne (4D4A0001) ~70 s; MechAssault 2 (4D53006B) ~50 s (BACK did not skip it either); Alien Hominid (5A440004) ~24 s.
- Expect up to ~60 s of logos before most titles and ~2 min in some: Midnight Club 3's title appears ~107 s after launch, Brute Force (4D53001E) ~100 s, Dead or Alive 3 (4D53002D) scrolls a copyright warning for up to ~3 min cold. Re-read rather than press.
- A loading screen whose spinner frame stays identical for over a minute is an emulator freeze (Mercenaries 4C410015): call it unknown, do not press.
## title_screen and attract mode
- Read the prompt: "Press START" wants START; A did nothing on Midnight Club 3's, Dino Crisis 3's (43430003) and DOA3's titles. Forza's says PRESS A TO START.
- Titles time out into attract demos with a full HUD: 187: Ride or Die (55530036) race, Crash: Wrath of Cortex (56550003) "DEMO" level, Azurik (4D530007), D&D Heroes (49470013), DOA3 fights, Bloody Roar: Extreme (48550001) "DEMONSTRATION". Not gameplay: press START (Azurik's demo still says "Press START to play").
- If START and A both leave a title unchanged after three tries it is a known dead end (AMF Bowling 2004 42530009; Psychonauts 4D4A0012). Untried there: BACK, the triggers.
## main_menu and submenu
- A accepts and B backs out in every title seen. START also selects on some menus (Galleon 41540004, JSRF 49470018, Buffy 45410012).
- Fastest to play: New Game, Quick Start (WWE Raw 2 5451000D), Quick hits (187), Instant Action (PGR2 4D53004B), Quick race (PGR 4D530003), Arcade (Forza; Midnight Club 3), Exhibition (DOA Xtreme 54430007).
- Avoid Xbox Live, System Link, Xbox Demos and <New Account>: they loop or leave the game (Conker 4D530051, Tron 2.0 42560001, PGR2). Avoid Training (BloodRayne) and vacation/shop modes (DOAX Zack Island).
- Prefer New Game to Continue/Load: Castlevania's Continue replayed the opening and returned to the title. Exception: Buffy, whose 10-save limit blocks Start Game.
- Move one step, re-read, then A. If nothing moved, switch between STICK:<dir> and UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT; if it jumped, step back. One flick moved 2-3 rows in Brute Force, Midnight Club 3 and Buffy.
- Auto-repeating menus stop at their ends: push to the end, then step back once (PGR2 to Instant Action; RalliSport Challenge 2 4D530039 to Time Attack).
- The highlight is usually the brightest item (Buffy's main menu, Baldur's Gate: DA 5655001A dialogue); BloodRayne marks the selected item DIM.
## profile_creation and name_entry
- A default name filled in and the cursor on DONE/OK/END/ACCEPT: press A once (Black, Burnout Revenge 45410076, Crash: WoC, Midnight Club 3, Grabbed by the Ghoulies 4D530053, Brute Force).
- Never mash A on a keyboard: it types (50 Cent 56550042 typed "a" forever, Black Stone 58490004 "AAAA", Castlevania "AAIIIIII").
- Cursor on a letter: type one letter with A, press START once, re-read. START jumps to Accept/END in Castlevania and Super Monkey Ball Deluxe (53450038) and is Done in Halo 2 (4D530064); it types a letter in Spikeout (53450029), RalliSport 2 and Black Stone, and does nothing in GoldenEye: RA and Battlefield 2: MC (45410062). Then walk to DONE/OK with the D-pad or the left stick, A.
- An empty name is refused: you stay on the keyboard (Castlevania; Shin Megami Tensei: NINE 41540002).
- Empty profile list: pick Create/New (Burnout Revenge's lit Load Profile only says "no profiles to load"; 187's slots read Empty). With profiles listed, take the lit one (Midnight Club 3 lights the last used).
## save_load_prompt and controller_prompt
- Read the highlight before A on Yes/No. On No by default: Castlevania create-save and overwrite, Burnout 3 "save your profile?" (A on No looped back). On Yes: SMB Deluxe, Sonic Heroes "Create Game Data 01?", Burnout Revenge autosave, Burnout (41430006).
- "Continue without saving" reaches play and leaves the disk alone (50 Cent: X; Crash Twinsanity). Otherwise Yes to a new save reaches play. "No save data found... press A" is just A (Alien Hominid, SMB Deluxe).
- Controller/settings screens: A takes the defaults (187 Controller Configuration, Forza assists). Join screens: A joins player 1; never START at "Press START to Join" (D&D Heroes; Arctic Thunder 4D570002, then DONE).
- Online sign-in: B skips it (Tron 2.0's footer "B Skip Xbox Live sign-in"); answer No to anything that leaves for the Dashboard.
## gameplay, pause, false passes
- gameplay = HUD up AND the scene answers STICK:up held ~2 s. A HUD alone is not proof: attract demos, Halo CE (4D530004) cryo training, a race grid at 0 mph (PGR, Forza until RT is held).
- Never press START in gameplay: it pauses (Sonic Heroes, Buffy, Family Guy 545400B0, THPS3 41560004, Gauntlet 4D57000E), opens Options (Dino Crisis 3) or a status screen (Castlevania). On a pause panel read the lit line: Otogi's (46530002) is Restart Stage (B closed it); D&D Heroes' A chose Save Game.
- A dimmed scene with a box or arrow is a tutorial waiting for A (Azurik, Ghoulies, Gunvalkyrie 49470017); Y skips all of Brute Force's; B turns off Battlefield 2's help.
- Not gameplay though often 60 fps: a level splash or goal list (THPS3 "THE FOUNDRY", THPS2x 41560001 HANGAR CHECKLIST: A), START MENU (Capcom Classics 2 43430019), Stage Select (SMB Deluxe), a shop list (DOAX), a LOAD/SAVE terminal (Crash: WoC), a storybook page (Ghoulies), CPU "WINNER/PERFECT" (KOF 534E0007), Game Over/Continue (Burnout), TIME IS UP (Arctic Thunder), RESULTS (Burnout Revenge).
- Driving: RT throttle, LT brake/reverse (PGR, Forza, Midnight Club 3, Burnout, Midtown Madness 3 4D53002A); A is the throttle in Arctic Thunder and 187.
- On-screen tips name the input to send (Halo CE "Use X to exit the cryo-tube"; Gauntlet "Press A Button when done").
- One unchanged frame for a minute with the HUD up is stuck but still gameplay: back off and turn (Sonic Heroes, Gunvalkyrie, 007: Agent Under Fire 4541000D); LT re-centres Buffy's camera from the sky.

## Sports (team and individual sports; the owner's 10-05 rule)
- Set the period length before the first whistle: in the options or game settings, the quarter, period, half, inning or game length to the LONGEST value offered, and the game clock to the slowest or real-time setting if offered. One period must outlast the hold, or the hold meets an end-of-period menu (owner rule, 10-05; a run that shows the defect gets a rerun with the period maxed).
- Choose a team a human controls: the controller icon under one team on team select (a team with no controller is CPU, and a match between two CPU teams is not play).
- A quarter, period, half or inning break, halftime, end-of-period stats, a replay card or a next-period prompt is period_break: START, then A to continue. Do not wait it out.
- ESPN / Sega Sports 2K team select (NBA 2K2/2K3, NHL 2K3, NFL 2K2/2K3): controller 1 is the top controller in the middle column. ONE RIGHT (or LEFT) moves it one notch into the side column next to the HOME (or AWAY) team, and a "User Name" plate appears above it with an arrow on one side only: that IS assigned. It does not travel under the team logo. Then START (no more LEFT/RIGHT, which undo it). (NBA 2K3, 10-05, frames 019 -> 020 of runs/nba-2k3: RIGHT assigned it, and 60 more presses undid and redid it.)
- Kickoff, tip-off, faceoff, serve or pre-snap play-call screens are menus until one press starts the play: A (American football: A to pick the play, then A to snap).
