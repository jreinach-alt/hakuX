# Ubisoft titles (5553xxxx)
Seen: 187: Ride or Die (55530036), Tork: Prehistoric Punk (55530040). Deathrow, Far Cry Instincts, Splinter Cell: Double Agent, XIII, Beyond Good & Evil, the Batman titles and the rest have no route evidence.
- Boot: the Ubisoft logo, then a "Press START" title from ~22-25 s to ~50-55 s after launch (187, Tork); after that the title gives way to an attract sequence. START while the title is up; press again if it stays (187 missed one short press).
- 187's attract is a race with a full HUD: not gameplay.
- 187: PLAYER PROFILE with Empty slots: A = Create; a keyboard with "N" lit: A enters it, Y validates and goes straight to the main menu.
- 187's main menu is a horizontal list (Story mode, Quick hits, Xbox Live, ...): one stick pulse left/right moves one item. Quick hits, Western Whip Race, a driver, a car (A each).
- 187, first race on a profile: Controller Configuration (Classic): A. Then a narrated tutorial clip that drives itself ("To accelerate big dog style..."): A skips it, START does not. The race starts ~8 s later.
- 187 in the race: hold A to accelerate (Classic layout); the left stick steers.
- 187 hung once on a black screen with a spinner at boot (1 boot in 3, after a fresh save): black with a spinner for over a minute is a hang, report it.
- Tork: left alone after its title, an in-engine cinematic runs at 1-4 fps; whether it is the intro or attract is not known. Past "Press START" nothing is recorded.
