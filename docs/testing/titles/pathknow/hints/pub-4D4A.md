# Majesco titles (4D4Axxxx)
Seen: BloodRayne (4D4A0001), Psychonauts (4D4A0012). BloodRayne 2, Aeon Flux, Blowout and Drake of the 99 Dragons have no route evidence.
- BloodRayne: the Terminal Reality and Majesco logos skip with A.
- BloodRayne's menus mark the selected item DIM: the bright entry is NOT the selected one. The left stick does not move them; the D-pad does.
- BloodRayne: New Game opens on "Training": DOWN to "Act 1 - Louisiana", A (Training also plays, but its tutorials interrupt control). Difficulty Normal (A); the control-scheme text (A); then DOWN twice to "Start Game", A.
- BloodRayne: after the load an in-engine opening with Mynce runs ~70 s and A does not end it; then Rayne on the grass below a church with health bar and compass: gameplay. Swamp water drains her health.
- Psychonauts: the title "Press START to begin" (credits scrolling behind it) did not advance under START and A every 5 s in two runs. Untried: a longer START hold, BACK. Call it unknown rather than pressing for minutes.
