# 4947xxxx titles (Dungeons & Dragons Heroes; canonical ids of JSRF and Gunvalkyrie)

Panzer Dragoon Orta (4947002B), Enter the Matrix, The Matrix: Path of Neo, Unreal Championship, Driver: Parallel Lines, Godzilla and the rest have no route evidence.
- D&D Heroes: Bink and publisher logos; with no press the main menu (NEW GAME lit) is up ~55 s after launch, then an attract demo (heroes with keys and potions already collected: not gameplay). A through the menus to the hero select.
- D&D Heroes hero select: FIGHTER lit (A). "Press START to Join" is for player 2: do not press START. "Name Your Hero" with ARADIN filled: A. A load with a "Types of Spells" card.
- D&D Heroes: a dwarves cutscene (~40 s warm, much longer cold) that A and START do not end: wait. Then ARADIN in the ruins with health and mana bars: gameplay. START in play opens Game Options (an A there chose Save Game); X opens a skill list.
- JSRF: Smilebit, Dolby and notice screens, a city intro, then "PLEASE PRESS START TO BEGIN", also shown over the attract loop. START leaves the intro, opens the menu and picks NEW GAME (first item, no save prompt).
- JSRF: the Garage dialogue with Corn ("This is the GG's Garage") advances with A; then Yoyo on skates in the Garage with health bar and spray cans: gameplay. START does nothing in the dialogue.
