Seen: 007: Nightfire (45410026), 007: Agent Under Fire (4541000D). GoldenEye: Rogue Agent: series-goldeneye.md.
- Nightfire: title "Press START" (START), gun-barrel intro (A), then the Paris prologue starts at once with no menu or profile: an on-rails sniper section, "Tutorial: Limited weapon control", auto-aim on. That is gameplay: RT fires and the shots move it on.

- Agent Under Fire: plain START/A rounds reached the first mission in first person (the decryptor). A forward-only stick loop then faced one vault door for 20 min: if the view stops changing, turn instead of pushing forward.
