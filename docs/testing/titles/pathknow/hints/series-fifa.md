# FIFA 2003-07, FIFA Street 1-2, World Cup 2006 (EA; 45410017 ... 454100A6, Thor)
No route on this emulator yet. Every line below is general knowledge of these games, unverified here:
replace a line with what the screen showed when one of these titles reaches play.
- Expect EA SPORTS and licence logos plus an intro video; A or START skips most of them. EA titles ask
  for a profile or save before the menu (pub-4541.md).
- Fastest to a match: Play Now, Kick Off or Friendly with the default teams. Avoid Career, Manager,
  Tournament and Online.
- The controller screen shows controller icons that move left or right under the home or away team. An
  icon left in the middle means CPU against CPU: the ball moves and the HUD is up, but the stick does
  nothing. Push STICK:left or STICK:right to put controller 1 under a team, then A or START.
- Team and kit screens: A or START accepts the defaults. A loading screen with tips follows.
- Gameplay is the pitch with a score and clock in a corner, with STICK:* moving the player who has the
  marker over him. A kickoff cut-in, a replay or a half-time menu is not gameplay.
