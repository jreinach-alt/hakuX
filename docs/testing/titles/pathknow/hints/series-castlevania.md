# Castlevania (Konami)
Seen: Castlevania: Curse of Darkness (4B4E002D), the only Castlevania in the library. Konami's ESPN 2002, Dance Dance Revolution, Winning Eleven and Frogger titles have no route evidence.
- Boot: the opening movie skips with A; the Konami logo goes black on its own. Left idle, the title replays the movie: A skips it again.
- Title New Game / Continue / Options: with a save on the disk the cursor starts on Continue: push up to New Game, then A. Do not press during the title's ~2 s fade-in: a press there takes the lit item.
- Continue replays the whole opening recap and returns to the title; only a second Continue reaches LOAD HARD DISK. Prefer New Game.
- Name Entry: the keyboard can take 10+ s to appear after New Game; wait for the "Name Entry" banner. A types the highlighted letter, START jumps the cursor to Accept, A accepts; "Is this correct?" Yes lit (A).
- "There is no save data ... Create new save data?" sits on No: push left onto Yes, A. Slot list: A on slot 1. "Previous save data exists. Overwrite this save data?" sits on No: left, A. "Start without saving?" (after a decline): left onto Yes, A still reaches play. "Please don't turn off your Xbox console." is the save in progress.
- The "1476, Valachia" crawl and castle shots: A skips each. Then a gothic courtyard with a gargoyle fountain and "Player" HP top left: gameplay. The HUD hides when he stands still.
- START in play opens the Player status screen (not gameplay): B closes it. Walking straight ahead pins him on the fountain: walk a square.
