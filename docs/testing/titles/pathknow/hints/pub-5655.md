# Vivendi Universal titles (5655xxxx)
Seen: 50 Cent: Bulletproof (56550042), Bruce Lee: Quest of the Dragon (56550016), Crash Twinsanity (56550036), Crash Bandicoot: The Wrath of Cortex (56550003), Baldur's Gate: Dark Alliance (5655001A). Buffy: Chaos Bleeds, The Simpsons Hit & Run, Hulk, Enclave, AvP: Extinction and the rest have no route evidence.
- These titles offer a way past saving: 50 Cent's "- Create New Profile -" screen has X: Continue without saving; Crash Twinsanity's "No saved games found" prompt has CONTINUE WITHOUT SAVING. Take it: 50 Cent's profile keyboard types "a" forever under A.
- 50 Cent: after X, "Are you sure ..." sits on No: push the stick up, A.
- 50 Cent: the Genuine Games and publisher logos skip with A; Title Screen New Game (A); Choose Difficulty, Thug lit (A); the no-save path above; the intro cutscene (A skips); then an alley at night with two allies: gameplay.
- Bruce Lee: intro (START), title "Press START" (START), New Game (A), then Player Info / Purchase Moves / Continue with Player Info lit. A on Player Info opens a status screen A never leaves (B backs out). Move down twice to Continue: the menu steps once per push, so hold the stick down ~2 s per step and re-read. Then a night video (START) and the dojo fight.
- Baldur's Gate: DA's logos play out by themselves (the menu is up ~60 s after launch).
