# ESPN NFL / NBA / NHL / College Hoops 2K5 (Sega, Visual Concepts; 53450030-53450033, Thor)
No route on this emulator yet. Every line below is general knowledge of these games, unverified here:
replace a line with what the screen showed when one of these titles reaches play.
- Expect Sega, Visual Concepts and ESPN logos, then a title screen that wants START.
- Pick the shortest match: a Quick Start, Exhibition or Play Now style entry. Avoid Franchise, Season,
  Online and Create modes: they open long setup screens.
- The controller screen shows controller icons that move left or right under the home or away team. An
  icon left in the middle means the CPU plays both sides: the match runs and looks like play, but the
  stick moves nothing. Push STICK:left or STICK:right once to put controller 1 under a team, then A or
  START.
- Pregame intros and presentation cut-ins skip with A or START. Gameplay is the field or rink with a score
  and clock HUD, and STICK:* moving the player who has the marker over him.
- When the four titles share a menu layout, a path that worked on one is the first thing to try on the
  others. pathfind records it in paths/.
