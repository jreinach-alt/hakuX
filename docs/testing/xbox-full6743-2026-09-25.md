# A complete silicon reference set at the pinned tests commit `6743b6a`

**Measured 2026-09-25 on the project console (GPU rev 163 / MCP rev 212).**
It was registered beforehand in
[`docs/lanes/xbox/full6743-prediction.md`](../lanes/xbox/full6743-prediction.md)
(`aa0a15bf29`, amended `74dd4d8f5a` before the console run). This is
idle-time console work at the owner's request, and it closes the reference
gap in #293.

## What ran

- **The XBE:** pristine nxdk_pgraph_tests `6743b6a` (sha256 `8612681afa58…`),
  the commit the NV2A index pins.
- **The tests:** 2,951, across all 102 index suites except the two that save
  nothing (`PVIDEO`, `Clipping precision`; #296). `RenderTextureLoop` was
  included as its own leg (#294).
- **The console run:** one run of 751 s. It returned 3,396 captures, logged
  "Testing completed normally", and handed back to the dashboard.
- **Emulator dry run first:** every test ran on a handheld through the
  dispatcher, in eight requests.
  - Part 3 hit the 1800 s disc timeout while still completing tests. W
    buffering runs at about 10 s a test there.
  - Its unreached tests were re-run in two W buffering chunks plus the tail
    suites.
  - Every piece completed with no crash signal.
  - The console's test list is the union of the dry runs' own `Starting`
    names.

## C1 (instrument)

- `Alpha func`: 16 of 16 bit-identical to the published goldens.
- `Fog planar vsh` (6) and `Surface as vertex array` (5): all bit-identical
  to PR #261's run of the same XBE.

- **The 16 captures with no golden** are the tests added at `6743b6a` (#293):
  `Fog planar vsh` 6, `Surface as vertex array` 5 and `Texture format` XAlpha
  5. They now have pristine silicon references.
- **`RenderTextureLoop`** is bit-identical to its golden. hakuX matches it
  exactly too (below), so the sweep's skip (#294) can go once the disc is
  `6743b6a`.

**For a sweep that moves to the `6743b6a` disc (#293):** score `Clear`
against these references, not the published goldens. Otherwise its 8
captures read as about 2.04 M px of residual that belongs to no emulator
change.
