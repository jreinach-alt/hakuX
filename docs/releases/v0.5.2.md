# v0.5.2

Performance fixes for **Halo: Combat Evolved** and **Mortal Kombat: Shaolin Monks**. This page also records the work since the official line's latest release, v0.3.1: this fork's v0.3.3-j1, v0.4.0-j1, v0.4.1-j1, v0.5.0 and v0.5.1.

> **GPU driver.** hakuX is tested only with the Turnip Adreno Driver T30
> (@Mr_Purple_666). The handheld's own driver and other Turnip builds are
> untested and draw some games wrongly.
> [Which driver, and how to install it.](https://github.com/jreinach-alt/hakuX/blob/home/docs/gpu-driver.md)

**Before installing:** it needs an Android handheld on Android 13 or later with an ARMv8.2 CPU with LSE atomics
(Snapdragon 845 / 865 class or newer). It installs over 0.5.1 as an ordinary update, and installs alongside an official hakuX build rather than
replacing it. Launching from ES-DE still needs the two files in
[`docs/es-de/`](https://github.com/jreinach-alt/hakuX/tree/master/docs/es-de).

## What is new in 0.5.2

- **Halo: Combat Evolved's opening cinematic runs much faster.** It held 17 fps through the hangar briefing on a
  Retroid Pocket Nova; it now runs at about 29 fps there. Three things were slowing it down: a slower texture path the
  cinematic's colour surfaces took every frame, the game waiting on the GPU to confirm each occlusion (visibility) test
  before moving on, and the CPU translator recompiling around one floating-point instruction more often than it needed
  to. One fix answers an occlusion test as soon as it is queued, using the previous frame's result; that can make a
  visual effect such as a flare show up a frame late at a camera cut. Some stretches still run under 30 fps. Tracked in
  [#1018](https://github.com/jreinach-alt/hakuX/issues/1018).
- **Mortal Kombat: Shaolin Monks' Goro's Lair cutscene runs about twice as fast.** It ran at 27.5 to 29 fps before and now
  holds about 54 fps on the Nova. The same change (16-bit render targets that a game samples as textures are
  now converted on the GPU instead of being downloaded to the CPU each frame) is on for every title.

## Everything since v0.3.1

Each version below lists its title-specific work first.

### v0.5.1 (2026-10-16)

**Fixes carried forward from X1 BOX.** hakuX descends from [X1 BOX](https://github.com/izzy2lost/xemu) by izzy2lost,
which brought xemu to Android. X1 BOX kept fixing bugs after hakuX forked from it, and this release pulls those forward:

- Bad audio data from a game no longer closes the emulator; the voice is skipped. Out-of-range ADPCM audio plays
  instead of producing noise, and a missing audio device is no longer fatal.
- A texture pointing outside video memory draws blank instead of aborting the emulator or reading past the end of
  memory.
- A corrupt pipeline cache is discarded and rebuilt instead of crashing the app at start.
- The setup wizard rejects a flash BIOS file that is not 256 KB, 512 KB or 1 MB.
- Renderer fixes: a staging buffer flushed before its copy, a uniform-buffer barrier that reaches the fragment stage, a refused null GPU command, and a vertex-array change that was not noticed after a DMA change.

Thank you to izzy2lost for the repository these came from.

**Titles**

- **Halo: Combat Evolved:** the main menu's ring no longer jumps back in bursts. Float-to-integer stores now round the
  way the Xbox does.

**Everything else**

- A new adaptive app icon with a themed layer; the app is now named **hakuX CE** (Community Edition).
- Launcher setup guides for ES-DE, Daijisho, Pegasus and the stock launcher.
- The compatibility list now covers 98 titles: 72 Playable, 15 that run but fall below 30 fps, and 11 that do not yet play.
- **Needs Android 13 or later** (0.5.0 needed Android 10).

### v0.5.0 (2026-10-12)

The first release rated by play rather than by pixel counts alone: **52 titles reach gameplay and hold it** (a timed run
with no crash, hang, audio dropout or hitch, at 30 fps or better for at least 90% of it) on a Retroid Pocket Nova or an
AYN Thor. Frame rates were measured on 175 titles.

**Titles**

- **Need for Speed: Most Wanted:** stuttering and repeated audio in movies and speech are fixed (the audio unit's
  bursts outran the game's writer; they are now paced). The first race after loading now starts at 29.8 fps, up from 26.4 fps.
  The warm race-start countdown now takes 34.22 ms a frame, down from 44.23 ms. The race start renders through the driver's
  system-memory path, which cuts GPU time per frame by about a quarter there.
- **GTA San Andreas:** hard-edged white polygons in the sky are fixed. About 5% less emulated-CPU work per frame.
- **RalliSport Challenge:** rival cars no longer vanish on alternate frames during the countdown and close passes.
- **Forza Motorsport** no longer slows down over the course of a race.
- **50 Cent: Bulletproof:** pre-rendered movies ran at 10 to 16 fps of their 30 and now play at full speed.
- **NBA Live 07:** the median frame rate rose from 23 fps to 47 fps.
- **007: Agent Under Fire:** gameplay rose from 17 fps to 26 fps. **Dead or Alive Ultimate** also renders through the
  system-memory path.
- **Crimson Skies:** the slowest tenth of frames rose from 23 fps to 26 fps (fewer wipes of the CPU's jump cache).
- **Galleon** and other titles that set their clock from VBLANK ran slow (Galleon's clock rose from 56.587 Hz to 59.816 Hz); fixed.
  Separately, a 720p or 1080i title was detected as PAL and timed at 50 Hz.
- **Conker, Blinx 2 and Forza** no longer empty the CPU's address-translation cache each time the GPU watches a surface
  (Conker: 145 full flushes a second down to 1).
- **Kabuki Warriors, Dead or Alive 3, Tron 2.0:** the ubershader removes the multi-second to minute-long freezes while
  an exact shader compiles on first play.
- **Arctic Thunder:** pipelines pre-built from a previous play avoid 4,661 ms of pipeline creation on a cold start.

**Everything else**

- **Save states:** ten slots per game plus an auto slot, undo for the last save and load, optional auto-save on quit.
- **Multiple games folders now supported**, shown together in the library.
- **Fast renderer paths** and **GPU surface reuse**, each a Graphics setting with a per-game override.
- **The AYN Thor's bottom screen** fills with a game instead of a 640x480 corner.
- Fixed: several crashes and hangs, including a translation-cache race with disc DMA and an overflow in the inline
  vertex path.
- Rendering accuracy, measured against a real Xbox's framebuffers: wide lines (47.82% to 99.58% match), YUV and DXT
  textures, signed blend equations, shadow-map comparisons, fog, blits, geometry clipping.
- Box art lookup is now **off until you turn it on**; the app tells you exactly what it sends.
- Needs Android 10 and an ARMv8.2 CPU with LSE atomics. (0.5.1 and later need Android 13.)

### v0.4.1-j1 (2026-09-26)

**Titles**

- **Grabbed by the Ghoulies, RalliSport Challenge 2, Conker:** the frame rate no longer collapses to 1-3 fps after a few
  minutes (Ghoulies now holds a median 29 fps on both devices).
- **Dead or Alive 3:** the frame rate rose from 2 fps to 52 fps. **Jet Set Radio Future:** from 27 fps to 57 fps.

### v0.4.0-j1 (2026-09-10)

**Titles**

- **Crimson Skies: High Road to Revenge:** skipping the intro video no longer closes the emulator. The game writes a
  texture description the hardware cannot decode while the video shuts down; the renderer now binds a blank texture
  instead. Skipping the opening movie and mission cutscenes was checked on a Retroid Pocket Nova.

**Everything else**

A rendering-accuracy release, every change measured against framebuffers captured from a real Xbox 1.0 (the
`nxdk_pgraph_tests` golden results): texture borders, border colour, cylinder wrap, signed texture channels, cube maps,
texture formats, depth, fog, fixed-function lighting, bump mapping, clipping, and a long list of crashes in the
renderer that no longer happen.

### v0.3.3-j1

The first release of this fork. No title-specific changes. It installs alongside an official hakuX build under its own
application id, adds an EEPROM export, and fixes launching a game from an external frontend such as ES-DE, which did
not work in any released build. Full history:
[CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/jreinach-alt/hakuX/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md).
