# v0.4.0-j1

A rendering-accuracy release. Every change below was measured against
framebuffers captured from a real XBOX 1.0 (the `nxdk_pgraph_tests` golden
results), most of it on a Retroid Pocket Nova, some on a desktop Vulkan lane.
The scoreboard in `docs/testing/` records each change with the exact pixel
counts before and after.

**Two things to know before installing:**

- It installs **alongside** an official hakuX build rather than replacing it,
  and upgrades a previous fork build in place. Nothing is uninstalled and no
  saves are at risk.
- Launching it from ES-DE still needs the two configuration files in
  [`docs/es-de/`](../es-de/).

### Rendering

- **Textures with borders.** A swizzled texture that carries its own border
  ring is stored at twice its size with the interior four texels in; the
  renderer created the image at the logical size and sampled the ring. 2D, 3D
  and cube-map borders are now exact, and a bordered cube face is remapped on
  the face the lookup selects. (`Texture_border`: 10 → 14 of 18 tests
  bit-identical; every 3D bordered test and cube faces up to 8×8 exact.)
- **Border colour.** A BORDER-mode axis returns the border-colour register as
  written, whatever the texture's format; Vulkan's sampler border colour went
  through the format's channel set and came back white or green. Decided in
  the pixel shader. (`Texture_border_color`: 56,419 → 16,205 differing px.)
- **Wrap modes and border colour written between draws** now rebind the
  texture; before, six wrap modes drew as repeat.
- **Cylinder wrap.** `SET_TEXTURE_ADDRESS`'s CYLINDERWRAP bits interpolate a
  coordinate the short way round the unit cylinder, on WRAP axes only, in the
  geometry shader. (`CylinderWrapping`: 18,953 → 2,778 px; both wrapped quads
  bit-identical.)
- **Texture perspective.** `SET_CONTROL0`'s texture-perspective bit is
  honoured; with it clear, colours and coordinates interpolate linearly in
  screen space.
- **Signed texture channels, one at a time.** The four per-channel signed
  flags signed the whole texel before. A partial set now signs only the
  flagged channels, and the scale is the hardware's: a signed 0x7f reaches
  the combiner as 254/255. (`Texture_signed_component_tests`: 1 → 17 of 19
  exact; all sixteen flag combinations 0 px.)
- **Textures the CPU rewrites in place.** A texture rewritten behind
  unchanged registers, rewritten several times in one frame, or rewritten
  while a stale copy of it sat unbound in the cache, drew its old texels.
  Four gates in the renderer and three gaps in the dirty tracking are closed.
  (`Texture_CPU_Update`: 0 → 2 of 2 exact; the 30 cube-map dot-product tests
  that sampled the suite's first cube: 56,909 → 3..80 px each; palette swaps
  and mid-frame gradient rewrites exact.)
- **Cube maps.** The constant-eye reflection mode (`DOT_RFLCT_SPEC_CONST`)
  is implemented, and the HILO_HEMISPHERE dot mapping completes the hemisphere
  normal the way `NV_texture_shader` defines it. (`Texture_cubemap`: 17 → 31
  of 72 exact; ten HiLoHemi tests from ~50k px each to 2..60.)
- **Texture formats.** `SZ_Y16`, `SZ_R16B16` and `LU_IMAGE_R16B16` are
  implemented; Y16 puts luminance in green and blue; R6G5B5 decodes unsigned
  with bit replication (exact on the render-target suite); every slice of a
  volume texture is converted; a texgen mode on a channel that lacks it
  disables the channel; float depth textures decode in the shadow comparison.
- **Render targets.** A surface textured through a different format is
  exact wherever the format decodes exactly; the remaining rows in that suite
  are one-pixel rounding. (Note: that suite's newest test poisons the rest of
  it on any hardware; `docs/testing/configs/` has the config that skips it.)
- **Depth.** The W-buffer slope offset is applied once per primitive at the
  quad the hardware uses (`W_buffering`: 7.2 M → 1.3 M wrong px); interpolated
  depth is floored exactly; 24-bit and float depth survive a readback round
  trip; a Z16 depth surface downloads through its depth aspect.
- **Fog.** The fog factor is computed per fragment from the three fog
  parameters the way the hardware does, with exp2 a Gaussian in bias and
  distance, and the linear factor truncated to eight bits. (Eight fog suites:
  13 → 23 of 280 exact, 13.95 M → 7.26 M px.)
- **Fixed-function lighting.** Spot and specular factors are evaluated with
  the lighting unit's rational function from the six specular parameters;
  colour material terms are sourced as the unit sources them; the lit
  specular folds into the diffuse when specular is disabled; two-sided
  lighting is implemented; the infinite light direction is used as written;
  inputs carry a 13-bit fraction and round as the transform unit does.
  (Eleven lighting suites: 0 → 27 of 195 exact, 7.17 M → 2.76 M px;
  `Lighting_Two_Sided` and `Material_color` fully exact.)
- **Bump mapping.** A bump environment map's channels are signed per flag the
  way the hardware signs them, and the alpha stays out of the luminance.
  (`Bump_map`: 1.65 M → 0.44 M px; `Bump_env_lum`: 4.21 M → 1.95 M px.)
- **Clipping.** The fragment shader recompiles when the window clip
  rectangles change (`Window_clip`: 54 → 92 of 92 exact on the desktop
  lane); a clear is bounded by the surface clip rectangle; a narrow-format
  surface rebinds from fresh VRAM after a download landed under it
  (`Surface_clip`: 15 → 29 of 47).
- **Vertex data.** A vertex RAM upload reaches every frame of the ring; the
  vertex shader rebinds when the attribute masks change.

### Stability

- Unimplemented surface formats, an undescribable pixel shader, a combiner
  stage count past eight, evicting the bound surface, invalidating a bound
  surface, a texgen mode without a channel, and a volume texture deeper than
  one slice all aborted the emulator. None of them do now; every colour
  surface format is mapped and the `Clear` suite runs to the end.
- Vulkan: descriptor sets are not recycled while the recording command
  buffer still uses them; texture and surface images retire behind their
  frame fence instead of being destroyed in use; an upload's transfer write
  is made visible before the surface is loaded or sampled; a draw into a
  surface with no pixels is skipped instead of creating a zero-extent
  framebuffer; a retired surface's image view is forgotten in every texture
  cache; a retired surface no longer writes over memory the guest took back;
  a deferred download retires the generation it captured. The renderer
  starts without FD memory interop, so it runs on more devices.
- Persisted shader module keys carry the build that wrote them; a file from
  another build is discarded instead of compiling the wrong module.

### Android

- A guest power-off exits the process (the main-loop lock is released so the
  shutdown completes).
- Debug builds have their own application id and label and install beside
  the release.
- Emulator stderr reaches logcat, so diagnostics are collectable.
- The setup wizard shows real progress while it copies an image.

### Tooling

- `docs/testing/`: a disc builder that enables suites or skips single tests,
  a scorer that separates bit-identical, off-by-one and blank results, a
  pixel-shader differ that compiles the generated GLSL, and one documented
  way to drive a device. The Linux desktop target builds and runs again and
  CI builds the Android app from a clean checkout.
- `docs/investigations/`: write-ups of the hardware behaviour found on the
  way (W-buffer slope offset, depth interpolation, R6G5B5 packing, cross-test
  contamination) and a handoff of what is still open.

### Known residuals

Recorded on the issue tracker with numbers: anisotropic filtering's far
field (vendor tap pattern), the YUV-to-RGB rule, DXT1 decode rounding, the
signed blend equations, a one-pixel column on one cube-map silhouette, the
second triangle of a quad sampling one texel off along the diagonal, and the
one-step rounding class that separates most remaining near-misses from
hardware.

### Notes

- Signed with this fork's own key. Releases from this fork upgrade in place
  with `adb install -r`; an official build is a separate app and is
  unaffected either way.
- Version names carry a `-jN` suffix so they cannot be confused with, or
  collide with, upstream releases.
