Run 2026-09-18 by `lane.nova54`, offline, over the 30 probe-on soaks already
on disk. **No device time, no new build, no new capture.** The two handhelds
came back into service the same morning and neither was needed.

Reproduce with:

```bash

```

## What the lane was dispatched to do, and why it did not need to

`[issue.54]`'s retirement condition:

> a soak with `-DHAKUX_VRAM_RACE_PROBE=1` retires the VRAM race probe

and `blocker_tested` said *"The retirement soak is not run."*

**Twenty-four such soaks were already on disk, on four titles, on both

session, between 11:14 and 11:40, as the queue resumed on its own. The probe
has been read off a title thirty times. The retirement condition is **met**,
and it was met before the holds lifted.

The reason nobody noticed is the same one recorded against this probe once
already -- `[issue.54]` itself says the counter *"had been running on Galleon
since `e353735028` with nobody reading its output"*. This is the second
instance, on the same instrument: the runs that carry it were queued for
**#44**, whose requests name `Tr` in their own purposes, so the output was
being generated by one issue's arms and was never pooled for the other's.

## The instrument's blind spot, established before any zero below is used

`profile.c:600` gates the pacing line -- the probe's only output -- on
`(frame_count % 60) == 0` in the flip path. So **the probe cannot be read off
a run that does not flip**, and an absent `Vr:` reads exactly like "no
races". (`[issue.54]` and `territory.toml` both cite `profile.c:479` for this
gate; the line has moved and the citation is stale. The gate is real.)

Every run used here produced **42 to 120 pacing lines**, i.e. 2,520 to 7,200
guest frames. The blind spot does not bite any reading in this document, and
`vram_race_probe_sweep.py` fails rather than reports when a run is too thin.

Two further controls, both passing on all 30 runs:

  * **The impossible row `Xd` reads 0 on all 30 runs.** `Xd` counts a range
    seen dirty and then seen clean by an immediate second scan, which nothing
    on this thread can cause. Non-zero would indict the instrument.
  * **Both denominators are per-EVENT, not per-second.** `Vr` is per
    vertex-range copy and `Tr` is per upload, so neither is a rate over a
    busy window and neither is diluted by device occupancy.

## The two registered legs, judged per title and per bound mode

`blocker_falsifier`, as registered:

> if "the read-side frequency is measured and the remaining question is only
> the probe's retirement" were false, a soak with
> `-DHAKUX_VRAM_RACE_PROBE=1` would report **Vr materially away from
> 5.45e-05** per instrumented read, **or Tr above the 5.35e-05 one-sided
> bound** -- on a title, not the disc, because the disc is zero at both sites
> and cannot discriminate.

Grouping matters and the grouping is not optional: **the probe and #44's skew
bound are independent knobs, and the bound removes the very window the probe
measures.** Pooling across bound modes mixes two populations. Refs
`d879e6e03b`, `7349193309` and `3b7fe5f5fa` carry the bound.

**On Galleon the registered figure reproduces.** Three runs have landed since
it was written (`t30b`, `t26`, `stock` at `c866527e03`, 415,557 further
reads, 24 further races); the pooled rate moves 5.45e-05 -> **5.54e-05** over
**9 runs, 4 refs spanning 280 commits, both devices**, at p = 0.45. That is a
real reproduction and it is the strongest thing in this document.

**It does not transfer.** Dead or Alive 3 runs at **7.31e-06**, 7.5x lower,
p = 4.3e-05. Crimson Skies, JSRF and the disc are all zero, though only DOA3
has the read volume to say so with power.

One honest caveat on the size of "materially away", because the leg never
stated a tolerance: Galleon's own **per-run** rates span 3.17e-05 to 8.86e-05,
a **2.8x spread over 9 runs**. So a leg reading "away from 5.45e-05" could
not have failed on Galleon for anything under roughly an order of magnitude.
DOA3 at 7.31e-06 is 4.3x below Galleon's lowest single run, which is outside
that band -- but it is outside a band nobody registered, and it should be read
as `p = 4.3e-05 against the pooled Galleon rate` rather than as a stated leg
failing.

And the 2.8x spread is **not** a device difference. It appears **within each
device**: nova 7.41e-05 (`-470`) against 3.94e-05 (`-413`), thor 6.39e-05
(`-298`) against 3.69e-05 (`-190`). Device and ref were confounded in the
registered six; adding the other three decomposes it off disk, and neither
factor survives.

## What this means for #54

**The probe can be retired. "The read-side frequency" cannot be quoted as a
number.**

The retirement condition -- a soak with the probe on -- is met thirty
times over. The probe has answered its question, has a passing impossible
row, and can be deleted rather than left switched off, which is what
`vk/draw.c`'s own comment asks for.

But what it measured is **two rates, each a property of the workload and of
the bound mode**, spanning four orders of magnitude:

```
VTX site   Galleon 5.54e-05   DOA3 7.31e-06   Crimson/JSRF/disc  0
TEX site   Crimson 0.608      Galleon <= 3.82e-05   DOA3/JSRF/disc  0
```

No title is high at both sites. Galleon races at the vertex site and never at
the texture site over 78,480 uploads; Crimson is the reverse over 16,704
reads. So `[issue.54]`'s summary -- *"every race at the VERTEX site"* -- is
true of Galleon and **false of the corpus**, and the single figure it is
attached to is a Galleon figure wearing a general label.

This is the file's own **test-disc-ratios** rule one level up. That rule says
a density measured on the disc is not the workload's. This says a density
measured on *one title* is not another title's -- already recorded for cost
(#44's `gfps` on Galleon against Crimson Skies) and now for a race rate. And
it is the project-memory note **"a bound is not a value"** landing on a live
number: `5.35e-05` bounds Galleon's TEX rate and equals nothing, and it was
then used as the bar for every title.

**Nothing here refutes the probe or the measurement.** Both legs are sound
readings of the counter. What fails is the scope of the claim the legs were
written to defend, and the correct repair is a scoped sentence, not another
run.

## A second finding, which belongs to #44 and not to #54

The same table settles something #44 has been asking about, and it needs no
further device time either:

**The skew bound zeroes both race sites.**

| | unbounded | bounded | expected in the bounded runs at the unbounded rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crimson TEX | 61,965/101,879 = 0.608 |  **0/83,945** (mode 1 and 2, 6 runs) | 51,057 |
| Galleon VTX | 20/270,080 = 7.41e-05 (nova, `-470`) | **0/177,501** (mode 2, 2 runs, same device) | 13.1, p = 2.0e-06 |

Mode 1 -- *"the cell the issue has never measured"*, per its own request
purpose -- reads `Tr` exactly 0 on both runs, and mode 2 now reads 0 on four,
including one at the tip-based `3b7fe5f5fa`. The Galleon vertex half is the
part nobody had looked at: the bound eliminates that site too, on the same
device, at p = 2.0e-06.

`lane.skew44` holds this and has a registered prediction over it
(`issue44-tip-crimson-tr-three-modes.json`, legs T1 and T2 both "Tr exactly
0"). **This lane has not judged that arm and must not** -- the numbers are
recorded here so they are not re-measured, and the verdict is that lane's.

## What could not be closed

  * **Galleon has never been run probe-on at a tip-based ref**, nor on mode 1
    at all. Deliberately not queued: `[issue.54]`'s retirement needs no such
    run, the falsifier is decided, and Galleon's Vr has held across four refs
    spanning 280 commits, which bounds ref-sensitivity without one. Queueing
    it would be idle capacity manufacturing an unconsumed measurement.
  * **Why the two sites are disjoint by title** is unexplained. Galleon makes
    ~160k vertex-range copies and ~8k uploads per 240 s; Crimson makes ~3k
    and ~19k. The two titles simply exercise different paths, but that is an
    observation about volume and not a mechanism for the rate, and it is held
    as an observation rather than registered.
  * **DOA3's 7.31e-06 rests on 2 events.** Sound as a bound, weak as a value.
