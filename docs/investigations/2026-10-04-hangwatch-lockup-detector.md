# A locked-up title is caught on telemetry in about 90 s, not waited out

Lane: hangwatch. Dispatched directly (harness defect; no tracker issue). Owner, 2026-10-04 ~13:55 PDT, on the
Whiteout (4B4E0001) screening claim that held the Nova for 15 min on one "LOADING ... TROJAN PARK" card:
"If the audio isn't changing and the screen isn't, that's a failure that needs investigating with telemetry, not
waiting around for 13 minutes."

## What was built

`docs/testing/hangwatch.py` (stdlib only; the selftest runner has no numpy). A HANG is three signals bad together for
`HANG_S` = 90 s:

| signal | source (emitter) | bad when |
|---|---|---|
| `still` | the screen, pathfind's own `changed()` (FPS corner masked) | change <= 0.01 per look |
| `quiet` | `hakuX-audio` "level L" lines (`hw/xbox/mcpx/apu/apu.c`, `APU_LVL_LOG`) | the non-silent 50 ms window count did not move over one 5 s report |
| `pinned` | `[rr425w]` idle_us and `[rr425]`/`[rr425pc]` (`accel/tcg/cpu-exec.c`, `JC425_LOG`) | idle_us <= 50 ms per 2 s, and one (cause, pc) takes >= 0.9 of the window's TB returns |

Two readings of the emitters that the calibration depends on:

- `level L` is cumulative since boot. Its first count after "windows" is the non-silent window count and the second
  (labelled "flat") is the silent count. "Quiet" is therefore a delta, not a level.
- `starve:` (hakuX-audiocap) is a 30-s heartbeat of host-side callbacks. It says the host sink is pulling, not that the
  guest is making sound, so it is not used.

A signal the log does not carry is unknown and never confirms a HANG. A HANG verdict needs all three.

Wiring (`docs/lanes/hangwatch/pathfind-hook.patch`, not applied: no grant on `docs/testing/titles/pathfind.py`):
- `hangwatch.start()` follows the screening run's logcat into `screen-logcat.txt`. Not `logcat.txt`, because `hold_play`
  truncates that file.
- `hangwatch.look()` runs once per screening look and once per hold look. On a trip it sends ONE A press, logs it as a
  probe (`hang.jsonl`), compares the screen before and after over `RECHECK_S` = 20 s, and declares only if the screen
  did not change and the telemetry stayed bad. Otherwise it logs `probe-cleared` and the next trip needs another 90 s.
- On a verdict: the logcat tail, the last 10 frame names, `dumpsys cpuinfo`, `logcat -b crash`, `verdict.json`, and
  `host-tools/failure_intake.py one <dir> --force` (class `hang`). `result` is `hang`, distinct from `gave-up`.
- The patched `pathfind.py` passes its own dry selftest (52 checks). The dry run has no logcat, so the live branch
  is covered by the hangwatch selftest only (15 checks, including the confirmed case, a press that moves the screen,
  and a press after which telemetry stops).

Across all 1162 logs, pinned+quiet reaches 90 s in exactly one file (the Tron 532476 hang). The largest
legitimate-looking value is 70 s. So HANG_S = 90 s sits 20 s above the largest value seen, and no value between 70 and
716 s was observed. That margin is thin, rests on one unresolved run, and should be re-checked as more runs accrue.

What the table shows about the signals:
- Audio alone is not a hang signal: Counter-Strike and Sonic Heroes sit silent for 600+ s with the guest not pinned.
- Pinned alone is not a hang signal: Midnight Club 3 pins one PC for 88 s with audio active.
- The conjunction is what separates the cases, and the screen half of the rule is what the data could not score.

## The Whiteout claim (`runs/screen-whiteout/claim`)

- No logcat was kept. The screening loop never started one (`logcat_start` is called only in `hold_play`), so the
  emulator's own tags for this run are not in any file. The brief's rr425/audio numbers come from the owner's
  reading of the device, not from a file I could open. The Whiteout score below is therefore partial.
- Frames (saved per look, 138 of them, scored with `classify.motion` on the FPS-masked grey): masked change is
  0.000 on every consecutive pair from look 15 onward (123 pairs at <= 0.01). The loading card is static from
  about t=146 s to t=898 s. The "scrolling ticker" in the brief does not register under this measure.
- The screen half alone would have tripped about 90 s after t=146 s, which is about t=236 s. The rule would have
  stopped the claim about 11 minutes early, if the pinned and quiet halves also held.
- `steps.jsonl` carries `changed: null` on every row. The loop sets `steps[-1]["changed"]` on the in-memory dict after
  `write_step` has already appended the line, so the file never records it. The frames are the only record.

## The two Tron 2.0 hangs (#672) are not one class

- Run `532476` (queued as the #672 ubershader check): one PC takes the returns, the audio is silent, the screen is
  static. This is the class the owner's rule and the detector describe.
- Runs `1790971658` and run 6 match the #672 lane's own notes (`docs/lanes/tronhang672/NOTES.md`): busy 100% with the
  last IDE wake, a call/ret indirect-branch loop inside chained TBs, `[rr425pc]` top entries that are interrupt paths
  (not one PC), `fifoskew kicks=0`, no GPU submission, and near-black frames. The detector's `pinned` signal reads 2 to
  4 s on them and misses. Their audio is silent for 226 to 446 s, so `quiet` alone is not enough to trip either.
- The owner's rule (frames and audio) would not have caught run 6 before about 510 s, when the frames went static.
