# Compatibility

This lists the Xbox titles that have passed this project's **Playable** bar.
It is not a full compatibility list: plenty of other titles boot and run with
visible graphical faults, and many have simply not been tried yet. See
[`KNOWN_ISSUES.md`](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md) and the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/jreinach-alt/hakuX/issues) for what is
tracked about titles not on this list.

## What "Playable" means

A title is rated Playable when a held run reaches real gameplay and stays
there for at least 10 minutes (longer for a handful of titles known to
develop problems slowly) with no crash, hang or unexpected exit; spends at
least 90% of that time in actual play rather than menus, loading screens or
cutscenes (its **play share**); holds at least 30 fps for at least 90% of the
time it is playing (the **fps bar**); plays its audio with no dropouts; and
shows no hitches or flicker — checked by reviewing the captured frames
themselves, not just the frame-rate counter (**frame review**). The rating is
tied to the handheld it was measured on, an AYN Thor or a Retroid Pocket Nova.

A Playable rating means a title clears that bar. It is not a claim that the
title is free of bugs, and a title's absence from this list is not a claim
that it fails to run — only that it has not cleared this bar yet.
