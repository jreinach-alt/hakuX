## MEDIUM 1 — CLOSED, and the bound was widened by 3%, not made vacuous

`docs/testing/line_priority_arms.py:150` — `changed_region()` now unions every
footprint model (`for rule in (False, True)`) instead of hard-coding the narrow
perpendicular one.

**Pass 1b's scenario** was: the next person judging an arm the way this file's
own docstring instructs (`--extent-rule`) meets a violated must-be-zero in the
`outside` column, and either burns a cycle hunting a scoping escape that does
not exist or learns to stop reading the column.

**It can no longer occur.** I ran the judged command verbatim on the two
result directories the `[job.arms]` comment names, under **both** rules:

```
python3 docs/testing/line_priority_arms.py \
    --a .../1789968297-arms-primpv13-base-2094212 \
    --b .../1789968297-arms-primpv13-fix-2094234 \
    [--extent-rule] --min-width 8 --max-width 63.875
```

`outside = 0` on all 28 captures under `--extent-rule`, and `outside = 0` on
all 28 under the default. The eleven-class table is identical under
`--extent-rule` to the one pass 1b measured and the one `NOTES.md` now
carries — `ALL 225570, 96.05% → 100.00%, moved 8920`, to the digit.

**The fix cannot move any other column, structurally.** `changed_region()` is
called at exactly one place (`:280`), its result feeds only
`scope[test] = (diff.sum(), (diff & ~reg).sum())`, and `scope` is read only to
print `outside`. `A!=B px` is `diff.sum()`, which never sees the region. So
"no other column moves" is a property of the call graph, not a measurement
that could have got lucky.

**What the widening costs, measured rather than asserted.** Pass 1b asked for
the rule to be threaded through; the remediation went further and unioned both
models, which is a strictly wider bound and therefore a strictly weaker check.
I recomputed both regions at every width in the window and counted `outside`
under each against the real capture pair. The `old` column is also exactly
what the threaded-rule fix pass 1b asked for would give in **default** mode,
since threading `extent_rule=False` reproduces the perpendicular region:

**The positive control the docstring claims, run rather than believed.** At
w = 63.875 the region is 76,383 px of 307,200 (24.86%, 230,817 px excluded) —

included coordinate `(236,232)` gives `outside = 0`. The widened bound still
reports a scoping escape.

**The soundness argument checks out too.** `(max + min/2) / L` as a function

derived-extent footprint contains the perpendicular one at every angle and the
union *is* the extent footprint today, as the docstring says. Writing it as a
union is what keeps it a bound if a third model is added.

## NEW-L1 — the docstring understates the alternative it rejects, by a factor of eight

`docs/testing/line_priority_arms.py:168-171`

> *"Threading the run's rule through, which is what that audit asked for, is
> not enough: it fixes `--extent-rule` and leaves the DEFAULT reading 1 and
> 2 px outside at w = 40 and 48"*

Threading `extent_rule` into `changed_region()` gives, in default mode,
exactly the perpendicular region — which is what the same docstring says,
four sentences earlier, made **17 of the window's 28 captures** non-zero. The
two sentences describe the same region and the same capture pair and disagree:
the residual is 1, 2, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 8, 8, 9, 8, 8, 8, 8, 8, 9, 9 px on
seventeen captures from w = 40 to w = 63.875, not "1 and 2 px at w = 40 and
48". The measurement is in the MEDIUM-1 table above.

**Severity LOW, and why it is not MEDIUM.** It is a statement about a rejected
alternative, in a comment; no shipped behaviour, no scored number and no leg's
verdict depends on it, and the error runs *against* its own argument — it
makes the union look less necessary than it is, so it cannot mislead anyone
into shipping the weaker region.

**Failure scenario.** Someone simplifying `changed_region()` — a reasonable
thing to want, since the union costs a doubled inner loop — reads "1 and 2 px
at w = 40 and 48", judges the union over-engineering for a two-pixel residual,
reverts to the threaded form, and seventeen captures go non-zero again on the
default rule. That is the exact cycle pass 1b already spent.

**Remediation: one sentence, not a cycle.** Replace the clause with "leaves
the DEFAULT reading 1–9 px outside on 17 of the window's 28 captures, w = 40
upward". This pass does not send the PR back for it — an auditor may not edit
a lane file, and the correction is on the record here, beside the file, in the
same fold.
