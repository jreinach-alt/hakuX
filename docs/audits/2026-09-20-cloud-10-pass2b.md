## L7 — CLOSED. The upper-bound step is stated, and it is measured, not asserted

`md:153-163` and `--parity`'s "Why Y8's count may be SUBTRACTED" paragraph now
say the missing half out loud: a colour-change count is prima facie a *lower*
bound, subtracting `n8` needs it to be an upper one, and the licence is that
the base cannot step two cells between adjacent columns. `base_gaps()`
(`:316-330`) measures it over the label-free rows only — which is the right
restriction, since glyph edges would report one- and two-column spacings that
belong to text.

910 of them 5 or 6, and the six exceptions are gaps of 2, 4 and 7 on rows 52
and 73 — the seam rows — identically in all four quads. Worst case 0.5 cells of
base index per column, against the 1.0 the argument has to exclude. The bound
stands and now carries its own derivation.

## L9 — NEW, LOW. `--control` closes with an exclusivity the three numbers above it deny

`bump_y16_class.py:539-541`. After printing `BumpEnvLum_Y16` vs
`BumpEnvLum_A8` as **4,872 px in columns 0..43, 4,770 in 44..102 and 5,880 in
103..167**, the tool concludes "so `Bump env lum` does respond to a changed
offset **in exactly the columns** where `Bump map`'s Y16 defect lives". It
responds in all of them, and hardest outside the band. The claim the control
needs — the band is not blind — is the weaker one, and the document states it
correctly at `md:264-270` ("differ by 4,770 px in columns 44..102 … (4,872 px
in 0..43, 5,880 px in 103..167). The band responds to a changed offset").

**Failure scenario.** A reader runs `--control` alone, takes the closing
sentence rather than the table two lines above it, and carries "the `Bump env
lum` offset response is banded 44..102 the way `Bump map`'s Y16 defect is" — a
structural agreement between the two suites that does not exist. It is
M2's shape (the tool's prose disagreeing with the page) at the smallest
magnitude: the refuting numbers are printed in the same paragraph. Pre-existing
since `1f0a5dfd5f`; neither pass caught it. One word: "including".

## L10 — NEW, LOW. The one per-row count in the remediated paragraph that does not say which side of the label it is on

`bump_y16_class.py:435-438` and `:450-452`, `md:189-192`. The remediation's own rule
(`py:80-92`) is that "every report that quotes a per-column extent or a per-row
boundary count has to say which side of it the number is on". The flat
sub-band's `9..12` is pooled over all 168 rows, label rows included — and the
glyph spans columns 44..90, so it overlaps 78..90 of that sub-band and both
*adds* edges and *masks* checker boundaries there. Measured label-free, it is
10..11 in every quad; both the 9 and the 12 come from label rows.

**Failure scenario.** A reader reproducing the sub-band argument restricts to
the 150 flat rows, as the surrounding sentence invites, and gets 10..11 rather
than the published 9..12 — a mismatch against the page with no defect behind
it. The argument itself is safe and the direction is conservative: it uses the
minimum, and including the label rows *lowers* the stated minimum from 10 to 9.
No number on the page is wrong. Say "9..12 over all rows, 10..11 outside the
label", or restrict it.

## L12 — NEW, LOW. "well under half a cell per column" is exactly half at the seam

`md:161`. The document concludes the L7 paragraph with "the base index advances
well under half a cell per column". The six exceptional gaps it has just

Nothing downstream changes — the step that matters needs `< 1` — so this is a
word, and the word the tool already uses.
