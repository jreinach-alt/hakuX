## What pass 1 asked for

- **`sync_vertex_ram_buffer` still updates first and records after.** It
  does. In the head's `vk/draw.c`, `vertex_range_gpu_stale(r, addr, size,
  true)` is the statement right after `pgraph_vk_update_vertex_ram_buffer(...)`,
  inside the `if (dirty)` block. The stale check (`record = false`) runs before
  the bitmap walk. Neither the lane nor master moved anything between pass 1
  and this head.
- **LOW-1 fixed or accepted.** Accepted, not fixed: no commit after pass 1
  touches `vtx_surf_gen[]`. So the pointer-reuse scenario can still happen as
  pass 1 describes it: a pruned binding, the same allocator block reused at
  the same address, an equal `draw_generation`, and an outside download
  before the next vertex draw. Pass 1's bound still holds. In that case the
  draw gets the pre-fix behaviour for that one draw, so it cannot give a
  result that master would not also give. That is LOW and does not block a
  fold. A follow-up that clears the entry when a binding is freed, or keys on a
  per-binding serial, would close it.
- **LOW-2 and LOW-3** (download cost when a live target overlaps an attribute
  range, and a list walk in front of the skip paths) are cost questions that
  no leg measures. They are unchanged and accepted as LOW.

## Re-checked independently

- **No surface stays stale forever.** The shelved path is the only one that
  could leave `draw_dirty` set with nothing to retire it: the in-range
  download skips a shelved surface when `!shelved_dirty`. Every place that
  sets `shelved_dirty = false` either follows a `download_surface_deferred`
  on that surface (`surface.c:417`, `3717`) or clears `draw_dirty` in the
  same block (`surface.c:1972-1973`). `pgraph_vk_download_surfaces_in_range_if_dirty`
  completes its deferred downloads before it returns (`surface.c:433-437`),
  so the record step after the update sees the retired state.
- **Table overflow.** With more than 64 overlapping bindings, a slot is
  overwritten. The surface that lost its slot then reads as a miss
  (`i < 0`, so stale), and that costs one extra copy. It is never a
  false "fresh".
- **Fold safety.** `git merge-tree` of the head with `origin/master`
  (`bc6b93495a`) is clean. The only master commit since the lane's merge
  that touches `vk/` paths is the #310 fold, and the tree diff shows no
  overlap with the lane's hunks. CI on the head is green (build ×2, check).

## Recommend
