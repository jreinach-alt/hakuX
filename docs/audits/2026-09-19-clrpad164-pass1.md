# Audit pass 1 — PR #172, `lane/clrpad164`: clear to the surface's pad-bit constant (#164)

**Auditor** `job.cloud` (claims no files; audit record only).
**Subject** PR #172, branch `lane/clrpad164`, tip **`9c559b3f4b`**, four commits
over its stated base **`11ddd94a66`**. `origin/master` is **47 commits** ahead

**Date** 2026-09-19. **Records** `2026-09-19-clrpad164-pass1.{md,json}`.

**0 HIGH. 0 MEDIUM. 4 LOW.**

The diff is +413/−99 over four files. The behaviour change is 53 lines in
`hw/xbox/nv2a/pgraph/gl/draw.c`: one new static helper and a two-arm `#ifdef`
at one call site. `docs/testing/nv2a_index.json` is a mechanical regeneration,
and the other two files are the registered prediction and the lane notes. In
the whole emulator the change can move exactly one quantity: the alpha value
handed to `glClearColor()` when a desktop-OpenGL build with
`GL_ARB_blend_func_extended` clears a surface whose colour format is
`X1R5G5B5_Z1R5G5B5` or `X8R8G8B8_Z8R8G8B8`. It goes from 1.0 to 0.0. Nothing
else in the diff can change a pixel.

I found no HIGH and no MEDIUM, so this PR takes no remediation from me. The
four LOWs are recorded because they are cheap to act on later, not because
anything here should wait for them.

## The one thing a reader of this PR must not get wrong

**The device arm did not contain this code, and the PR's `verified` label is
about a binary that could not have carried it.** The lane says so itself, at
length, in the PR body and in `NOTES.md`; I checked the claim rather than
taking it, and it is correct — the change sits inside `#ifndef __ANDROID__`
and the verdict header gives each arm an `apk`. So `better 0 worse 0 same 74`
and "every checked capture byte-identical" are what an arm reports when the
patch is not in either binary, and the 104 legs that passed are all
`must_not_move`/`must_not_regress` expansions, which an all-identical arm
satisfies trivially. That is this repository's inert-control case and it
discharges nothing.

This does not stop the fold: `docs/testing/jobs/fold.sh:387` says in terms that
being `verified` is **not** a requirement and that only a live FAIL stops a
PR. What it means is that the fold decision here rests on **reading plus green
CI**, and on the two prior measurements this change is the third instalment
of (#59 on Vulkan, #158 on the GL raster half) — not on the label. I recommend
folding on that basis, and I say so knowing the measurement is missing.

## What I checked and found correct, so nobody re-derives it

Everything below was checked against the tree at the PR tip, not against the
PR's description of it.

* **The mapping is right, and the `_O` case really is a no-op.**
  `pgraph_get_clear_color()` (`pgraph.c:4650-4675`) has no alpha case for any
  of the four pad formats: all four fall to `default: *a = 1.0f`. So
  `PSH_PAD_ALPHA_ONE → 1.0f` changes nothing (which is why the `_O` twins have
  been bit-exact), `PSH_PAD_ALPHA_ZERO → 0.0f` is the whole change, and
  `X1A7R8G8B8_{Z,O}` — which take alpha from bits 24-30 of the clear value
  (`:4657-4667`) — are classified `PSH_PAD_ALPHA_NONE` by
  `pgraph_glsl_surface_pad_alpha_mode()` (`glsl/psh.c:254-266`) and are
  untouched. The two `_O` pins and the two `X1A7R8G8B8` pins in the prediction
  are therefore real model checks and not padding.
* **The state it reads is the right state.** `gl/renderer.h:335-345` names the
  three formats that look alike and says to "ask the register when the surface
  you mean is the current target". A `CLEAR_SURFACE` target is the current
  target, so `pg->surface_shape.color_format` is correct here — and it is the
  same expression the raster half reads at `gl/draw.c:522-524`, which is what
  the comment claims. It also matches where Vulkan ended up: `vk/draw.c:894`
  reads `pg->surface_shape.color_format` after the #89 narrowing away from
  `drawn_format`. One derivation, read three times.
* **The write actually lands.** `glColorMask` keys alpha off
  `NV097_CLEAR_SURFACE_A` (`gl/draw.c:304`), `Clear::TestSurfaceFmt` pushes
  `NV097_CLEAR_SURFACE_COLOR` alone
  (`clear_tests.cpp:329`) and that constant is `0xF0`, which contains
  `1 << 7`. The host surface holds the bit: `X8R8G8B8_Z` → `GL_RGBA8`,
  `X1R5G5B5_Z` → `GL_RGB5_A1` (`gl/constants.h:389-391`).
* **The clear colour survives to the `glClear()`.** `glClearColor()` is issued
  at `:313`, `pgraph_gl_surface_update()` runs at `:316`, and `glClear()` only
  at `:393` — so anything in `surface_update` that sets the clear colour would
  silently discard this change. The three other `glClearColor()` call sites in
  the GL renderer are `render_surface_to()` (`surface.c:1290`, reached only
  from `pgraph_gl_render_surface_to_texture`, not from `surface_update`) and
  the two `android_surface_download_*_to_guest()` helpers (`surface.c:2040`,
  `:2202`), which are `#ifdef __ANDROID__` and so cannot run in a build that
  compiles the new helper at all. Not clobbered.
* **Nothing else consumes the value.** `pgraph_get_clear_color()` has exactly
  two callers in the GL renderer and both are the two arms of the new
  `#ifdef` (`gl/draw.c:309`, `:251`). There is no second GL clear path.
* **The prediction's pinned keys all exist, and the `SFC_`/`SCF_` correction
  is right.** `kSurfaceFormatTests` (`clear_tests.cpp:38-47`) spells exactly
  two entries `SFC_` — `SFC_A8R8G8B8` and `SFC_X1R5G5B5_Z1R5G5B5` — while the
  `_O` twin is `SCF_X1R5G5B5_O1R5G5B5`; `run-2026-09-10-clear-adreno.tsv`
  agrees. Every one of the nine `must_not_move` globs matches at least one
  real capture (`Clear/C0*` alone matches 24), so no leg is inert for want of
  a name. Both named movers exist under the spellings the prediction uses.
* **The two suites pinned bit-identical really are clear-free on a pad
  surface**, which is the leg most likely to fail a future honest arm and the
  one I most wanted to disprove. `Surface_format` clears via `PrepareDraw` at
  `surface_format_tests.cpp:145` and binds the pad format afterwards at `:153`
  → `:230`, restoring `SCF_A8R8G8B8` at `:244`; `Blend_surface` clears at
  `blend_surface_tests.cpp:287`/`:494` before `RenderToSurfaceStart` at
  `:299`/`:500`; and `NV2AState::RenderToSurfaceStart`
  (`pbkitplusplus/src/nv2astate.cpp:1610-1655`) pushes no `CLEAR_SURFACE` of
  its own. The `Clear/C0*` pins are safe for the same reason —
  `TestColorMask` (`clear_tests.cpp:236-267`) clears the default framebuffer
  surface and never binds a pad format.
* **`nv2a_index.json` is a pure line-number regeneration.** Parsed both
  versions: `issues`, `method_regs`, `suites`, `symbols` and `unread` are
  byte-identical; the only differences are `loc` line numbers in 56 `sites`
  entries and 6 `gaps` entries, all in `hw/xbox/nv2a/pgraph/gl/draw.c`, all
  shifted by +49 (above the call site) or +53 (below it) — exactly the 49-line
  helper and the 4-line call-site block. `provenance.tests_commit` is
  unchanged at `91a0de45…`, so no suite could have been silently dropped. Two
  fields did change beyond line numbers; see L1.
* **The display path does not turn this into a visible-window defect.**
  `pgraph_gl_render_display` copies the surface into the display texture with
  `out_Color.rgba = texture(tex, texCoord)` (`gl/display.c:215`) and the
  display texture inherits the surface's alpha-carrying internal format
  (`:452-456`), so alpha 0 does propagate. It is not new with this diff:
  #158's raster stamp already writes alpha 0 into every *drawn* pixel of a
  `_Z` surface on master, so a compositor sensitive to the window's alpha
  would already show it over a drawn frame. The marginal exposure here is the
  cleared-but-never-drawn region. No golden sees host-window alpha, which is
  why I name it rather than pin it.
* **CI is green on the tip**, not only on the sha the PR body names: `build`
  ×2 and `check` all SUCCESS on `9c559b3f4b` (2026-09-20T00:03–00:10Z), and
  both Android and desktop configurations build, so both arms of the new
  `#ifdef` compile.
* **`Files:` matches the diff** (`git diff --stat 11ddd94a66...HEAD`), and the
  prediction is committed with refs that are ancestors of the tip. The two
  commits after `b_ref` touch only `docs/`, so `b_ref` names the right binary.

## Findings

### L1 — the index regeneration rewrites two host-absolute paths, and they flip

`docs/testing/nv2a_index.json`, `provenance.tests_root` and
`provenance.support_dirs`: `/home/user/nxdk_pgraph_tests` →
`/home/justin/nxdk_pgraph_tests`, same for `pbkitplusplus`. This is not the
lane's doing — `nv2a_index.py:524-526` writes whatever absolute path the
regenerating host used — but it is the lane's commit, and the field has now
alternated across ten regenerations on master: `b112e93bf8` user,
`ffe744a17e` justin, `9bb6f107b5` user, `8ce27b87fc` justin, `425094304f`
user, `927ca58506` justin, `5e1aafe756` justin, `7ffcd2bce8` user,
`11ddd94a66` user, and now `2ffd961764` justin.

*Scenario.* Two lanes that each shift a line in `hw/xbox` must each regenerate
the index, and if they regenerate on different hosts their diffs collide on
these two lines for reasons that have nothing to do with either change — a
conflict in a 100k-line generated JSON, at fold time, with no semantic content
to resolve it by. Meanwhile a reader diffing two index versions has to check
whether a provenance change is a real provenance change or a change of desk.

*Remediation (not this PR's).* `nv2a_index.py:524-526` should record the path
relative to the repository root, or a bare marker, and keep `tests_commit` —
which is the field the gate at `:544-583` actually reads — as the provenance
of record. Worth a lane of its own; no reason to hold #172 for it.

### L2 — the call-site `#ifdef` is wider than the GLES exclusion needs

`gl/draw.c:216` and `:308-312`. The helper is excluded from GLES builds by
preprocessor and the call site is split into two arms. Every symbol the helper
uses — `pgraph_glsl_dual_src_pad_supported()`,
`pgraph_glsl_surface_pad_alpha_mode()`, `PSH_PAD_ALPHA_*` — is declared in
`glsl/psh.h` and compiled on Android, and the flag's setter is inside
`#ifndef __ANDROID__` (`gl/renderer.c:204-228`), so on GLES the helper would
return at its first statement having done nothing. An unconditional definition
and a single unconditional call would be behaviour-identical on both
platforms.

This matters only because the comment at `:241-248` justifies the guard by
citing the raster half, and the raster half's own comment (`gl/draw.c:499-511`,
the sentence at `:509-510`) gives a different and stronger reason: *"the tokens
themselves are absent on GLES, not merely unwanted"* —
`pad_write_color_factor()` genuinely does not exist there, so that
site has no choice. Here there is a choice, and the H1 consistency argument is
about the *form* of the exclusion, which a plain runtime gate would not break.

*Scenario.* None, which is why this is LOW. The cost is that obtaining the
clear colour now has two call sites, one of which CI compiles and no golden
measures, so a future change to how the clear colour is derived has a second
place to forget.

### L3 — "which the Vulkan copy does not need to be" is not established

`gl/draw.c:234-239` says the capability gate is something *"the Vulkan copy
does not need to be"*. Vulkan is not gated, but not because it does not need
to be: `pgraph_vk_get_clear_color()` (`vk/draw.c:894-907`) applies the pad
constant unconditionally while the Vulkan raster path takes its pad mode from
`pgraph_glsl_dual_src_pad_supported() ? … : NONE` (`vk/draw.c:638-640`) and
the Vulkan readback override is gated on the same flag (`vk/texture.c:1439`).
On a Vulkan device without `dualSrcBlend`, Vulkan therefore gets exactly the
clear-writes-the-pad-constant-while-the-raster-does-not split that this
comment says the GL gate exists to prevent, with the operands swapped.

The blast radius today is zero — every Vulkan device in this harness supports
`dualSrcBlend`, including lavapipe — so this is a claim in a comment and
nothing more.

*Scenario.* A later lane reads "Vulkan does not need it" as a measured
asymmetry between the backends, and either drops the gate when porting the
next half or skips checking Vulkan on a device that lacks the feature. The
honest sentence is "Vulkan is not gated, and nobody has checked whether it
should be".

### L4 — the change is, in this harness, unfalsifiable, and the label says otherwise

Covered above and stated fully by the lane itself; recorded as a finding so it
survives into the audit trail rather than only into a PR body. The registered
prediction's machine-read half is `must_not_move` (9 globs) and
`must_not_regress: Clear/*`; `expect` and `expect_counts` are empty by
deliberate choice, each for a reason I checked and agree with (the 98,342 /
49,274 figures are golden-versus-golden and no arm can move them;
`Surface_pitch::Swizzle` moves on its own and would fail a `worse=0` leg for
reasons unrelated to this change). The consequence is that **no possible arm
verdict distinguishes this change from a no-op**: a correct patch and a
deleted patch both produce "nothing moved".

*Scenario.* If `pgraph_glsl_surface_pad_alpha_mode()` gained a row that this
switch did not, or the gate were inverted, the harness would report PASS. I
read the mapping closely (see above) precisely because nothing else is going
to.

*Remediation.* Not this lane's: the desktop-OpenGL arm the prediction asks for
is unreachable from a lane sandbox, and the arms job builds apks. When a
desktop-OpenGL A/B does become available, the two `expect` rows to add are
`Clear/SCF_X8R8G8B8_Z8R8G8B8` and `Clear/SFC_X1R5G5B5_Z1R5G5B5` — both must
improve — and the run must confirm two things before its zero is believed:
that it really ran OpenGL and not Vulkan-on-lavapipe (`AGENTS.md:1063`, #29),
and that `pgraph_glsl_dual_src_pad_supported()` was true.

## Outcome

0 HIGH, 0 MEDIUM → no remediation. Four LOWs, none of which asks this PR to
change: L1 belongs to `nv2a_index.py`, L2 and L3 are a guard and a sentence
that a later touch of this file can absorb, and L4 is a measurement no actor
in this harness can currently produce.

**There is nothing for pass 2 to verify** — pass 2 exists to confirm that
pass-1 scenarios can no longer occur, and no scenario here was handed back for
fixing. Label moved straight to `fold-ready`, with the fold decision resting on
reading and green CI and explicitly **not** on the `verified` label.
