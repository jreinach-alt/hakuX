## M1: the fourth TB-return cause and the counter that cannot separate it

Scenario: lane.jcache425 builds the loop-side counter the NOTES specified,
it books `gen_eob`'s `exit_tb(NULL, 0)` returns (POPF/STI/IRET, the kernel
IRQL idiom) as lookup misses, and a jump-cache change is aimed at returns no
lookup ever made.

Verified closed, in every place the spec is read:

- **NOTES "Why the TBs return"** now lists four causes. Cause 4 names
  `gen_eob()` and the same instruction set pass 1 cited. Checked against
  `target/i386/tcg/translate.c` on this head: `gen_eob` reaches
  `tcg_gen_lookup_and_goto_ptr()` only for `DISAS_JUMP && !inhibit_reset`,
  otherwise `tcg_gen_exit_tb(NULL, 0)` (the single-step and RECHECK_TF arms
  also end without a lookup). The NOTES states that causes 1 and 4 look the
  same at `cpu_exec_loop`.
- **The counter spec** now puts the cause-1 miss counter inside
  `helper_lookup_tb_ptr`, and counts cause 4 separately (a per-TB flag at
  `gen_eob`'s `exit_tb` arm, or the returning TB's last guest opcode). Only
  causes 2 and 3 and `interrupt_request` stay at the loop, where they are
  distinguishable. A cause-4 exit can no longer be booked as a miss.
- **The fps conclusion** no longer reads "a handful of PCs" as a poll; it says
  cause-4 PCs look the same, and that cause-4-dominated returns are a
  translator-EOB case, not #425's cache.
- **The PR body** lists all four causes and says the miss must be counted in
  `helper_lookup_tb_ptr` with the translator exits counted separately.
- **The hand-off where it is read**: a correction is posted on #425
  (2026-09-27T02:04Z) naming the fourth cause and the split counter, after
  the original hand-off (01:45Z). The NOTES records it.
