# Code of conduct

_Last updated 2026-10-16, for version 0.5.1._

This applies everywhere in this repository: issues, pull requests and their
comments.

- Be respectful. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Stay on topic, and assume good faith.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content or personal attacks, and no
  posting anyone's private information.
- No piracy. Do not post, share or ask for games, BIOS files, boot ROMs or
  hard disk images, or links to them. [Getting your Xbox files](https://github.com/jreinach-alt/hakuX/blob/master/docs/xbox-files.md)
  explains how to make them from your own console.

## Reporting a problem

Open the **⋯** menu on the comment, issue or pull request, choose **Report
content**, then **Report to repository admins**. Only the maintainer sees the
report.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock a conversation, or
block someone from the repository. Most problems end with a reminder.
