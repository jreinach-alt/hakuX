## Goal
Decide whether a shader-side model reproduces silicon. Read the test (nxdk_pgraph_tests
texture_anisotropy_tests.cpp) for the texture, the plane and the per-pixel derivatives; from the goldens for
Anisotropy-2/4/8 recover the effective (footprint, sample count, LOD bias) silicon used per row -- as regions
(rows y 245..364 across the full width), not points. Candidate models: N taps along the major axis with a
LOD derived from the minor axis; NV2A's own documented rule if any public source states one.

## Falsifier
Render each candidate offline (numpy, per-pixel derivatives from the quad's geometry) and score it against the
three goldens; the control is Anisotropy-1, which must stay exact under the model. If no candidate beats the
current capture on the level-2 golden by more than half its differing pixels, the answer is measured-negative:
say so, and the issue becomes `unmodellable`-shaped (the board applies it, not you).
