# #13 -- line-mode (wireframe) polygon rasterisation: offline rule derivation (cloud)

Issue: #13. Base: master ab39609a2d. Wave 221, job.board. Class: cloud (no device, no NDK).
Files: docs/lanes/cloud-13/** only. Analysis and a NAMED hunk, no edit under hw/.
       glsl/geom.c is held by lane.wparamcode223 (PR #321, fold-ready) and
       prim_rewrite.c is free: the hunk goes in NOTES for the lane that takes the grant.

## Goal
The 2026-09-25 inventory comment on #13 adds 57,686 structural px of line-mode
polygons outside the Line-width suite: Front_face `FrontFace_LM_*` (12 captures,
36,796 px), Shade_model `ProgLM_*` (24, 11,178), 3D_primitive plain lines (12,
9,712). Front_face scores only 24 of its 36 goldens. Determine from the goldens
and our captures on disk whether ONE rule (extent, cap/join phase, or edge order
for the wireframe path) explains all three families, and what it is.

## Method
Read the issue, its tracker row (`git show origin/board:nv2a_issues.toml`) and the
PR #194 NOTES (docs/lanes/*/NOTES.md) for what edge order already landed. Score
per line segment region, not per pixel: compare the golden's stroke extent and
end phase to ours on 4-6 captures per family, then fit a rule on one family and
predict the other two with the rule held out.

## Falsifier
The rule is fitted on FrontFace_LM_* only. It must predict ProgLM_* and the plain
3D_primitive lines to within the stated per-region tolerance with no refit. If it
predicts one family and not the others, say which and stop: that is two causes,
not one, and #13 splits.

## Done when
docs/lanes/cloud-13/NOTES.md gives the rule, the held-out scores, the must_move
and must_not_move captures, and the named hunk (file, function, the change) for a
later lane. No unmeasured claim: a figure not derived from a golden on disk is
labelled a bound, not a value. Comment the result on #13 and open the PR.
